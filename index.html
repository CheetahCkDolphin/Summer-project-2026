NSDA Unified Rules Summary

PUBLIC SPEAKING EVENTS
Original Oratory (OO)

A persuasive speech written by the student that attempts to convince or inspire. Maximum duration: 10 minutes (600s) + 30s grace. Exceeding 10:30 prevents a 1st-place rank. No more than 150 words of direct quotations allowed. Memorized (off-book). Visual Aids: Not Permitted.

Informative Speaking (INF)

An informative speech written by the student designed to explain or describe a topic. Maximum duration: 10 minutes (600s) + 30s grace. No more than 150 quoted words allowed. Memorized (off-book). Visual Aids: Permitted (Optional 2D/3D props up to 30s setup time; no live animals or weapons).

Declamation (DEC)

Delivery of a speech previously given by another public figure, preceded by an original student intro. Maximum duration: 10 minutes + 30s grace. Open to 9th/10th grade or middle school. Memorized (off-book). Visual Aids: Not Permitted.

Expository Speaking (EXP)

A 5-minute original informative speech explaining a topic or process. Maximum duration: 5 minutes (300s) + 30s grace. Memorized (off-book). Visual Aids: Permitted (Optional visual aids allowed).

LIMITED PREPARATION EVENTS
United States Extemporaneous Speaking (USX)

Contestants draw a question on US domestic policy, prepare for 30 minutes, and deliver a 7-minute speech (+30s grace). Key rules: 5-10 oral news citations required. Delivered from memory or brief notes. Visual Aids: Not Permitted.

International Extemporaneous Speaking (IX)

Contestants draw a question on foreign affairs and international policy, prepare for 30 minutes, and deliver a 7-minute speech (+30s grace). 5-10 oral news citations required. Visual Aids: Not Permitted.

Impromptu Speaking (IMP)

Contestants have a total of 7 minutes (+30s grace) combined for both preparation and speaking on a drawn prompt (quote, word, abstract topic). Delivered from brief notes. Visual Aids: Not Permitted.

INTERPRETATION EVENTS
Dramatic Interpretation (DI)

Solo performance of a single published dramatic script. Maximum duration: 10 minutes + 30s grace. Memorized (off-book). Focus on character vocal splits, dramatic pacing, emotional depth. Visual Aids/Props: Not Permitted.

Humorous Interpretation (HI)

Solo performance of a single published humorous script. Maximum duration: 10 minutes + 30s grace. Memorized (off-book). Focus on comedic timing, character popping, and physical comedy. Visual Aids/Props: Not Permitted.

Duo Interpretation (DUO)

Two-person performance of published literature. Maximum duration: 10 minutes + 30s grace. Off-stage focal point (no direct eye contact except intro), no physical contact permitted. Visual Aids/Props: Not Permitted.

Program Oral Interpretation (POI)

Multi-genre thematic program interweaving selections from at least TWO of Prose, Poetry, Drama. Maximum duration: 10 minutes + 30s grace. Handheld Manuscript Binder REQUIRED. Visual Aids: Not Permitted (except manuscript binder).

Prose Interpretation (PROSE)

Performance of published prose literature (short story, novel excerpt, essay). Maximum duration: 5 minutes + 30s grace. Handheld Manuscript Binder REQUIRED. Visual Aids: Not Permitted.

Poetry Interpretation (POETRY)

Performance of published poetic literature (single poem or collection). Maximum duration: 5 minutes + 30s grace. Handheld Manuscript Binder REQUIRED. Visual Aids: Not Permitted.
